Tablet / LAN access

Your tablet and PC must be on the same network. If Windows asks, allow Sidecast through the firewall on your trusted network. Guest Wi-Fi may block connections between devices. After changing networks, turn sharing off and on to refresh the link. Audio stays on your configured output.

Ready in less than a minute.

  1. Copy URLChoose Chat for chat and compact alerts. Add Alerts separately for fullscreen alerts.
  2. Add a browser source in OBSIn Sources → + → Browser, paste the URL. Chat: 400 × 650. Alerts: your full canvas size, for example 1920 × 1080. Select Both to use both sources together.
  3. Position. Test. Go live.Keep the background transparent. Sidecast must stay running; the desktop overlay can be hidden.
